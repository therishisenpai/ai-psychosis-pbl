AI Ethics & Cognitive Analysis

AI Psychosis

Excessive reliance on generative AI systems may induce psychosis-like cognitive and behavioral deviations in humans, particularly among digitally native generations.

Problem Statement

The rapid adoption of generative AI in education, decision-making, and emotional interaction has outpaced understanding of its cognitive consequences. Users increasingly defer reasoning, memory, and emotional processing to AI systems, normalizing hallucinated outputs and weakening independent thought.

Literature Review

Studies from MDPI, NAEP, NYT, and independent research indicate declining reading comprehension, reduced attention span, and increased AI dependency among students.

Research Gap

Existing work focuses on AI hallucinations at the model level. This study examines deeper human-side cognitive distortion, emotional reliance, and behavioral degradation.

Research Methodology

Input Sources

Academic papers, educational reports, generational studies, and behavioral observations.

Framework

Conceptual classification of AI-induced cognitive deviation based on dependency intensity and usage context.

Documentation

VIEW REPOSITORY

Analysis & Observations

Nature of Results Qualitative

Observations indicate increased cognitive offloading, reduced problem-solving initiative, emotional dependency, and normalization of AI-generated hallucinations among heavy AI users.
