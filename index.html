AI Psychosis: An Analytical Study

This project is part of the Project-Based Learning (PBL) curriculum. It focuses on the study of AI psychosis, where generative AI systems exhibit hallucinations, delusional reasoning, or inconsistent cognitive behavior.

Introduction

Recent advancements in large language models have led to the emergence of unexpected behaviors such as hallucinations and reasoning inconsistencies. These behaviors resemble certain cognitive distortions observed in human psychosis.

Problem Statement

There is a lack of structured frameworks to analyze, classify, and understand psychosis-like behaviors in modern generative AI systems.

Objectives

  • To study the phenomenon of AI psychosis
  • To analyze hallucination and behavioral deviation in AI systems
  • To propose a conceptual analytical framework
  • To discuss ethical and safety implications

Proposed Methodology

  • Literature review of existing research
  • Case study analysis of AI hallucinations
  • Conceptual framework development
  • Discussion of mitigation strategies

Expected Outcome

The project aims to provide structured analytical insights into AI psychosis and contribute to safer and more reliable AI systems.

Project Status

Currently in the research and documentation phase.
