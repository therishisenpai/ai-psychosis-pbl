AI ETHICS & COGNITIVE ANALYSIS

AI PSYCHOSIS

Excessive reliance on generative AI systems may induce psychosis-like cognitive and behavioral deviations in humans, particularly among digitally native generations.

Introduction

Recent advancements in generative AI and large language models have introduced unprecedented convenience, automation, and accessibility to information and decision-making processes.

However, emerging observations suggest that prolonged and excessive dependence on AI systems may be associated with hallucination-like reasoning, emotional reliance, and reduced independent cognitive effort.

Problem Statement

There is currently a lack of structured academic frameworks to analyze, classify, and understand psychosis-like behavioral and cognitive deviations emerging from excessive AI usage.

Objectives

  • To study the phenomenon of AI-induced cognitive dependency
  • To analyze hallucination and behavioral deviation in AI systems
  • To examine emotional reliance on conversational AI platforms
  • To propose a conceptual analytical framework
  • To discuss ethical and safety implications

Proposed Methodology

  • Literature review of existing academic and industry research
  • Qualitative analysis of AI hallucination case studies
  • Observational study of user behavior trends
  • Conceptual framework development
  • Discussion of mitigation strategies

Analysis & Observations

Observations indicate increased cognitive offloading, reduced problem-solving initiative, emotional dependency, and normalization of AI-generated inaccuracies among heavy AI users.

Note: The findings presented are qualitative and observational in nature. No primary statistical data collection was conducted as part of this phase of the project.

Expected Outcome

The project aims to provide structured analytical insights into AI psychosis and contribute toward the development of safer, more reliable, and ethically designed AI systems.

Project Status

Currently in the research, documentation, and conceptual framework development phase.
