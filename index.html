AI Psychosis: An Analytical Study

Exploring cognitive degradation, emotional dependency, and behavioral deviation caused by prolonged interaction with Generative AI systems

Introduction

The rapid advancement of Generative Artificial Intelligence and Large Language Models (LLMs) has significantly altered human interaction with technology. AI systems are increasingly used for learning, decision-making, and emotional engagement. While these systems offer convenience and efficiency, excessive reliance has raised concerns regarding cognitive and psychological impact.

What is AI Psychosis?

AI Psychosis is an analytical concept describing psychosis-like cognitive and behavioral deviations observed in humans due to excessive interaction with AI systems. This includes reduced independent reasoning, emotional dependency, hallucination acceptance, and distorted perception of reality. It is not a medical diagnosis but a framework for study.

Problem Statement

There is currently a lack of structured frameworks to analyze and understand the psychological and cognitive consequences of prolonged AI dependency. As AI systems become increasingly integrated into education and daily life, the absence of awareness and regulation poses significant risks, particularly for younger generations.

Objectives

  • To study the phenomenon of AI psychosis and its manifestations
  • To analyze emotional and cognitive dependency on AI systems
  • To identify behavioral deviation patterns in AI users
  • To discuss ethical and safety implications of AI-human interaction

Proposed Methodology

  • Literature review of academic and industry research
  • Analysis of case studies related to AI hallucination and dependency
  • Conceptual framework development for classification
  • Discussion of mitigation and ethical guidelines

Expected Outcome

The project aims to provide structured analytical insights into AI-induced cognitive deviation and raise awareness about responsible AI usage. The study is intended to contribute to safer, more ethical AI-human interaction models.

Project Status: Literature review and documentation phase in progress.
