AI Psychosis

This project examines the cognitive and behavioral effects of excessive reliance on generative AI systems, with a focus on reduced independent reasoning, emotional dependency, and decision-making fatigue among students.

Problem Statement

As generative AI tools become deeply integrated into academic and personal workflows, concerns are emerging regarding declining independent thinking, increased cognitive offloading, and emotional over-reliance on AI-generated interactions. These effects remain underexplored from a behavioral and ethical standpoint in undergraduate academic research.

Literature Review

Prior research highlights reduced attention spans, increasing dependence on algorithmic assistance, and the psychological impact of immersive digital platforms. Studies related to AI hallucinations and online behavioral dependency provide the theoretical foundation for this project.

Research Gap

While AI performance metrics are widely studied, fewer academic works address the long-term cognitive and emotional consequences of sustained AI usage among students, particularly at the undergraduate level.

Project Status

The project is currently in the research and documentation phase. Initial literature review, background analysis, and conceptual framing have been completed as part of the mid-term evaluation process.
