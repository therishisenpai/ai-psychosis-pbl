AI Psychosis

This project explores how increasing dependence on generative AI systems may influence human cognition, decision-making, and emotional behavior, particularly among digitally native users.

Problem Statement

With the rapid adoption of AI tools in education and daily life, concerns have emerged regarding reduced independent thinking, over-reliance on automated systems, and emotional attachment to AI-generated interactions. Currently, there is limited structured discussion around these effects in academic settings, especially from a behavioral and ethical perspective.

Literature Review

Existing studies highlight declining attention spans, increasing cognitive offloading, and emotional reliance on digital systems. Research on short-form content, AI hallucinations, and online behavioral dependency provides foundational insight for this project.

Research Gap

While technical performance of AI systems is widely studied, fewer works focus on the long-term cognitive and psychological impact of sustained AI dependence on users, particularly students.

Project Status

The project is currently in the research and documentation phase. Initial literature review, conceptual framing, and background analysis have been completed as part of the mid-term evaluation.
