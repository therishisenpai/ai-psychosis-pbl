AI ETHICS & COGNITIVE STUDY

AI PSYCHOSIS

With AI becoming part of everyday life, there is growing concern that excessive reliance on generative systems may be influencing how people think, decide, and emotionally respond—especially among younger, digitally native users.

Introduction

Generative AI tools have rapidly moved from experimental systems to everyday utilities. Students, professionals, and even children now interact with AI on a daily basis for learning, decision-making, and emotional expression.

While these systems provide clear benefits, recent discussions suggest that overuse may lead to reduced independent thinking, overtrust in AI outputs, and difficulty functioning without algorithmic assistance.

Problem Statement

Despite increasing dependence on AI systems, there is limited academic work that frames these behavioral changes in a structured and analytical manner, particularly from a cognitive and ethical perspective.

Objectives

  • To explore how prolonged AI usage affects human cognition
  • To examine emotional and decision-making dependence on AI systems
  • To study hallucination-like behavior in generative models
  • To develop a simple conceptual framework for analysis
  • To highlight ethical and safety concerns

Proposed Methodology

  • Review of academic literature and industry reports
  • Observation of user behavior trends and case examples
  • Qualitative comparison of AI-assisted vs independent reasoning
  • Conceptual analysis rather than numerical experimentation

Analysis & Observations

Initial observations suggest increased cognitive offloading, emotional reliance on conversational AI, and a tendency to accept AI outputs without verification among frequent users.

Note: This phase of the project is exploratory in nature. The analysis is qualitative and based on observation and literature, rather than primary statistical data collection.

Expected Outcome

The study aims to contribute a clearer understanding of how AI dependency may influence human cognition and to encourage responsible and balanced use of generative AI systems.

Project Status

The project is currently in the research and documentation stage, with further analysis planned in later phases.
