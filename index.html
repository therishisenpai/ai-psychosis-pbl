PROJECT BASED LEARNING (PBL)

AI Psychosis

An analytical study on the cognitive and behavioral impact of excessive dependence on generative AI systems.

Introduction

Generative AI systems have rapidly become integrated into education, entertainment, and personal decision-making. While these tools provide convenience and efficiency, increasing dependence on AI raises concerns regarding reduced independent thinking and emotional reliance.

Problem Statement

There is limited structured research examining the psychological and behavioral effects experienced by users due to prolonged interaction with generative AI systems, particularly among students and digitally native populations.

Objectives

  • To study cognitive dependence arising from AI usage
  • To analyze behavioral and emotional reliance on AI systems
  • To examine hallucination acceptance in AI-generated outputs
  • To discuss ethical and educational implications

Methodology

  • Literature review of academic papers and reports
  • Observation of user behavior patterns
  • Qualitative analysis of documented case studies
  • Conceptual framework development
This study is qualitative in nature and does not rely on experimental or numerical datasets.

Analysis & Observations

Observations indicate increased cognitive offloading, reduced problem- solving initiative, emotional reliance on conversational AI, and growing trust in AI-generated information without verification.

Expected Outcome

The project aims to provide a structured understanding of AI-related cognitive dependence and encourage balanced, responsible usage of generative AI systems.
