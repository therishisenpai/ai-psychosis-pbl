# AI Psychosis: An Analytical Study

This project is part of the Project-Based Learning (PBL) curriculum.

## Introduction
The project explores the phenomenon of AI Psychosis, where large language models exhibit hallucinations, delusional reasoning, or inconsistent cognitive behavior.

## Problem Statement
To analyze and understand the causes, manifestations, and implications of AI psychosis in modern generative AI systems.

## Proposed Methodology
- Literature review of existing research
- Case study analysis of AI hallucinations
- Conceptual framework for classification
- Discussion of mitigation strategies

## Outcome
A structured research framework and analytical insights into AI psychosis.

## Status
Research and documentation phase.
